Phase 1 · UX Research
Empathy Map
Synthesized from 57 survey responses — primary user: CS/AI student researcher
Thinks
"There are too many papers — I can't keep up with the field."
"I wish I could just ask a question and get the right papers instantly."
"Math notation in papers is intimidating and slows me down."
Feels
Overwhelmed by the volume and pace of new publications.
Frustrated that current tools force too many tabs open.
Excited about AI tools — already uses ChatGPT daily.
Says
"I want semantic search, not just keyword matching."
"I start on Google Scholar then just ask ChatGPT to explain the paper."
"Mobile support matters — I read on my phone on the go."
Does
Tabs between Google Scholar, ArXiv, ChatGPT for every session.
Copies text from PDFs and pastes into ChatGPT to understand equations.
Spends under 3 hours/week on literature review — surface-level only.
Pain points
No way to see how papers connect — can't identify research gaps.
Can't consume content while multitasking (no audio mode).
Citation formatting is manual and error-prone.
Goals / Gains
Find the most relevant papers for a topic in under 2 minutes.
Understand complex papers without needing a separate AI chat.
Auto-generate citations in APA/IEEE — zero manual work.
